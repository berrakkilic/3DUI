# Bard’s Lost Flute
A small Unity adventure game.

## Story
After a long and rather drunken night, a bard wakes up to discover that his beloved flute is missing.
The bard must explore the village and talk to NPCs to gather clues. His search eventually leads him to a mysterious wizard, who provides a magical potion containing a portal spell.
The potion transports the bard to a dangerous creature that has stolen the flute. After defeating the monster, the bard recovers his instrument.

## Features
* **3DoF Navigation and Movement** – First-person exploration of the environment using dance mat.
* **Wayfinding Support** – Multiple navigation aids including compass-based guidance, breadcrumb trails, landmarks, and NPC-provided directions.
* **Dialogue-Based Interaction** – Interactive NPC conversations used to communicate quest information and navigation cues.
* **Object Selection and Interaction** – Context-sensitive interactions with game objects and characters throughout the quest.
* **Environmental Exploration** – Navigation through a virtual village and surrounding areas to locate objectives.
* **Portal-Based Teleportation** – Instant transportation between locations using a magical portal mechanic.
* **Raycasting Combat Interaction** – Player-triggered bow fight with a monster.
* **Object Collection and Manipulation** – Retrieval of the quest items.
